Japanese Maki Sushi

Description

Making sushi at home is easier than you think! These simple maki rolls are filled with fresh salmon, cucumber, and avocado, wrapped in seasoned rice and nori.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • Nori (seaweed) sheets
  • Fresh sushi-grade salmon
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Soy sauce and wasabi for serving

Steps

  1. Cook the sushi rice and mix it with a little rice vinegar, sugar, and salt. Let it cool.
  2. Place a nori sheet on a bamboo mat and spread a thin layer of rice over it.
  3. Add slices of salmon, cucumber, and avocado in a line across the rice.
  4. Roll the mat tightly to form a cylinder.
  5. Slice the roll into bite-sized pieces with a sharp, wet knife.

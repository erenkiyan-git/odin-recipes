This lasagna recipe is practical enough to prepare after a busy day and delicious enough to serve to your guests. The perfect harmony of layers of dough, a generous meat sauce, and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 package lasagna sheets
  • 500 gr ground beef
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cups tomato puree
  • Grated cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Dice the onions and sauté them lightly with the minced meat.
  2. Add the tomato puree and spices and cook until it thickens into a sauce.
  3. Arrange the lasagna sheets and the meat sauce in layers in a baking dish.
  4. Sprinkle plenty of grated cheddar cheese on top.
  5. Bake in a preheated 180°C oven for about 25 minutes, or until the cheese is golden brown.

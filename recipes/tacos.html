Mexican Street Tacos

Description

Authentic and quick. These street-style tacos feature marinated steak, fresh cilantro, and onions served on warm corn tortillas with a squeeze of lime.

Ingredients

  • Small corn tortillas
  • 1 lb flank steak (Carne Asada)
  • 1 white onion, finely chopped
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Limes
  • Spicy salsa

Steps

  1. Marinate the steak in lime juice, garlic, and Mexican spices for at least 1 hour.
  2. Grill the steak over high heat until cooked to your liking, then chop it into small pieces.
  3. Warm the corn tortillas on a dry skillet.
  4. Fill each tortilla with the chopped steak, onions, and cilantro.
  5. Serve with a lime wedge and a spoonful of salsa.
